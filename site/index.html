About

ArthaMerge is a boutique advisory firm exclusively focused on sell-side M&A, secondary liquidity, and consolidation-led exits for India's growth-stage D2C brands.

We operate at the intersection of capital, strategy, and execution — working selectively with founders and early investors who are ready to structure a meaningful transition. Our mandate is singular: delivering outcomes, not activity.

Services

What We Do

01

Sell-Side M&A Advisory

End-to-end transaction management — from positioning through to deal closure — representing the interests of founders and shareholders seeking a strategic exit.

02

Secondary Liquidity Solutions

Structured liquidity events for early investors and employee shareholders — enabling partial or full exits without disrupting ongoing operations.

03

Consolidation & Roll-Up Transactions

Facilitating mergers and roll-up strategies within fragmented D2C categories, creating scale and synergy for all stakeholders.

04

Exit Readiness & Positioning

Pre-transaction advisory to strengthen financial, operational, and narrative positioning — ensuring the business commands the valuation it deserves.

Process

Our Approach

01

Assessment & Readiness

A candid evaluation of the business's exit-readiness — financials, structure, narrative, and timing — before any transaction process begins.

02

Valuation & Positioning

Establishing a defensible valuation range and crafting the transaction narrative that resonates with the right class of buyers or investors.

03

Buyer Mapping

Discreet, targeted outreach to a curated universe of strategic acquirers, consolidators, and financial buyers — quality over volume, always.

04

Transaction Structuring

Negotiating and structuring deal terms — consideration, earnouts, warranties, and governance — to protect client value at every turn.

05

Execution & Closure

Coordinating due diligence, legal, and regulatory workstreams to bring the transaction across the line — with discipline and without distraction.
