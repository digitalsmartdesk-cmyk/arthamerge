## User

Design an elegant, minimal website for ArthaMerge, a boutique strategic M&A advisory firm based in India that helps growth-stage D2C (Direct-to-Consumer) companies and early-stage investors achieve structured, value-maximizing exits.
Tone & Positioning: Quiet confidence, not flashy. Think boutique investment bank meets modern editorial design — the kind of site that signals discretion, precision, and high-trust advisory work rather than a typical SaaS or startup site. Avoid generic tech/corporate clichés (no stock photos of handshakes, no blue gradients, no rocket-ship icons).
Aesthetic direction: Elegant and simple — generous whitespace, restrained color palette (warm neutrals like stone/cream background, charcoal/ink text, a single refined accent color such as bronze, terracotta, or deep olive used sparingly). Serif headline typography paired with a clean sans or mono for labels/details. Minimal ornamentation — no drop shadows or clutter for their own sake, no underlines beneath headings. Let typography, spacing, and hierarchy do the work.

What We Do — four services: Sell-Side M&A Advisory, Secondary Liquidity Solutions, Consolidation & Roll-Up Transactions, Exit Readiness & Positioning.

Target Deal Profile — key metrics (EV ₹10–75 Cr, Revenue ₹8–50 Cr, Stage 3–7 yrs, Founder-led/VC-backed) plus sector focus: Beauty & Personal Care, Nutrition & Wellness, Packaged Foods, Home & Lifestyle Brands.

Overall goal: The site should feel like it belongs to a firm that turns away more deals than it takes — precise, unhurried, and quietly premium.
